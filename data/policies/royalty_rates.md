# Royalty Rate Schedule for Minor Minerals in Odisha

## Applicable rules
Royalty rates for minor minerals are notified by the State Government under
Section 15(3) of the Mines and Minerals (Development and Regulation) Act, 1957,
and revised periodically. The rates below are illustrative of the prevailing
schedule applicable through the i4MS system.

## Dead rent
Where a lessee does not carry out any mining operations during a quarter, dead
rent is payable at the rate specified in the lease deed or auction terms. The
lessee pays the higher of royalty or dead rent for each quarter.

## Payment procedure
1. Royalty is calculated on the quantity dispatched (as recorded in e-Permits
   and e-Transit Passes) multiplied by the applicable rate.
2. Payment is due within 30 days of the end of each quarter.
3. Payment can be made online through the i4MS portal (preferred) or via a
   treasury challan uploaded to the portal.
4. Late payment attracts simple interest at 24% per annum on the outstanding
   amount from the due date.

## Excess extraction
If the quantity dispatched exceeds the quantity permitted, the competent authority
may recover the value of the excess mineral (royalty plus penalty) and may
initiate suspension or determination of the lease.

## District Mineral Foundation (DMF)
In addition to royalty, an amount equivalent to 30% of the royalty is payable to
the District Mineral Foundation for the welfare of areas and people affected by
mining.
