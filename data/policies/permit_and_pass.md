# e-Permit and e-Transit Pass — Procedure and Validity

## e-Permit (Form L)
An e-Permit authorises a lessee or licensee to remove and dispatch minor
minerals from a source. The lessee first raises, dresses, stacks, grades and
analyses the mineral, then applies to the circle Deputy Director of Mines (DDM)
declaring the intended quantity, consignee and destination. The system runs
automated statutory checks before issue: Environmental Clearance validity,
State Pollution Control Board consent (Consent to Operate), forest clearance,
surface-right clearance, mining-plan validity, lease working status and the
minimum production quantity. After approval the system issues the permit with a
unique number, the permitted quantity, and a validity period.

## e-Transit Pass
The e-Transit Pass is the per-vehicle document that tracks a single consignment
from source to destination. It can only be generated by a lessee or licensee who
already holds a valid e-Permit; a pass cannot be raised against an exhausted or
expired permit. Each pass carries a unique 1D and 2D barcode containing the
vehicle and consignment details, which is scanned at check gates and railway
sidings for verification en route.

A pass cannot be generated for an unregistered vehicle: the mineral-carrying
truck must already exist in the registered-vehicle database. While generating a
pass, dispatch details are auto-filled from the permit; the operator supplies
only the carrier number, transporter name and check-gate name.

## Validity window
An e-Transit Pass is issued with a fixed validity window appropriate to the
distance between source and destination, after which it expires automatically.
A pass that is used, expired, or flagged at a check gate cannot be reused.
Movement of mineral without a valid pass is treated as illegal transportation.

## Crusher units
Stone crusher units must register on the i4MS portal. A crusher's registration
remains valid until the expiry of its siting criteria or its Consent to Operate,
and renewal must be applied for through the portal at least 30 days before the
registration expires. Transport of minerals from a crusher unit also requires a
valid e-Transit Pass obtained through the portal.
