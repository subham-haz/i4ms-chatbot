# Enforcement, Inspections and Penalties

## Inspection powers
The Director of Mines, Deputy Directors, and Assistant Directors of Mines (or
officers authorised by them) may at any reasonable time enter any mine, quarry,
crusher unit, stockyard or mineral transit point to inspect compliance with the
Act, Rules and lease conditions.

## Check-gate verification
e-Transit Passes carry a unique 1D and 2D barcode. At designated check gates
and weighbridges, an operator scans the barcode to verify:
- The pass is valid (not expired, used, or flagged).
- The vehicle number matches.
- The quantity on the pass does not exceed the permit limit.
- The route is consistent with the declared destination.

If any discrepancy is found, the pass is flagged as "Flagged" and the vehicle
is detained for further inspection. The check-gate operator cannot un-flag a
pass; only the circle DDM or higher authority can clear the flag.

## Common violations
1. **Transport without a valid pass:** The mineral, vehicle and equipment may
   be seized. A penalty of up to five times the royalty value may be levied.
2. **Overloading:** Quantity on the vehicle exceeding the pass quantity by more
   than 5% triggers a flag and seizure of excess mineral.
3. **Expired Environmental Clearance (EC) or Consent to Operate (CTO):**
   Operations must cease immediately. Fresh permits cannot be issued until the
   clearance is renewed.
4. **Non-filing of statutory returns:** Returns overdue by more than 30 days
   may trigger suspension of the lease. After 60 days, the authority may issue
   a show-cause notice for determination.
5. **Royalty default:** Non-payment beyond the due date attracts 24% annual
   interest. Persistent default (two consecutive quarters) may lead to
   determination and forfeiture of security deposit.

## Seizure and confiscation
Seized mineral is stored in a government depot and, if not claimed within 60
days, may be auctioned. The proceeds are credited to the State Mineral Fund
after deducting dues and penalties.

## Appeals
Any person aggrieved by an order of the Tahasildar may appeal to the Sub-
Collector within 30 days; orders of the Sub-Collector may be appealed to the
Collector; and orders of the Collector to the Mines Tribunal or the State
Government, as applicable.
