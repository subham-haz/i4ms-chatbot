# Registration Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)

## Lessee / Licensee registration on i4MS portal
1. The applicant visits the i4MS portal and clicks "New Registration".
2. Fills in personal details: name, PAN, mobile number, email, Aadhaar (optional
   but recommended), category (Lessee / Licensee / Crusher).
3. Uploads supporting documents: PAN card copy, trade licence, lease deed
   (if already granted), proof of address.
4. Pays the one-time registration fee through the integrated payment gateway.
5. The system issues a temporary registration number pending verification.
6. The circle Deputy Director of Mines (DDM) verifies the documents and either
   approves or returns the application with remarks within 7 working days.
7. On approval, the applicant receives a permanent Registration ID and can
   log in to apply for permits, generate transit passes, and file returns.

## Crusher unit registration
1. The crusher owner registers as a "Crusher" category on the i4MS portal.
2. Uploads: factory licence, siting approval, Consent to Establish from SPCB,
   Consent to Operate (CTO), land ownership / lease proof.
3. The system validates CTO validity dates and flags if CTO expires within 30
   days.
4. Registration remains valid until the earlier of: CTO expiry, siting criteria
   expiry, or licence cancellation.
5. Renewal must be applied at least 30 days before registration expiry.

## Vehicle registration for mineral transport
1. The lessee or transporter registers each mineral-carrying vehicle on the
   portal.
2. Required details: vehicle registration number, RC book copy, vehicle type,
   carrying capacity (tonnes), and transporter name.
3. An unregistered vehicle cannot be used in an e-Transit Pass; the system
   blocks pass generation for unknown vehicle numbers.
4. Vehicle details can be updated (e.g. change of transporter) by the lessee
   through the portal.

## User roles and access levels
- **Lessee / Licensee / Crusher:** Can view own leases, raise permits, generate
  passes, file returns, and make payments.
- **Circle Officer (DDM / ADM):** Can view all leases and operations within their
  assigned district, approve permits, and flag non-compliance.
- **Directorate Admin:** Full access to all records across the state for policy
  analysis, audit, and enforcement.
- **Check-gate Operator:** Read-only access to verify transit passes scanned at
  the gate.

## Password and account security
- Passwords must be at least 8 characters, including one uppercase letter, one
  digit, and one special character.
- Two-factor authentication (OTP to registered mobile) is mandatory for officer
  and admin accounts.
- Accounts are locked after 5 consecutive failed login attempts for 30 minutes.
