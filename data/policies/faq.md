# Frequently Asked Questions — i4MS Portal

## General

**Q: What is i4MS?**
A: i4MS stands for Integrated Minor Mineral Mining Management System. It is an
online platform developed by the Government of Odisha for end-to-end management
of minor mineral operations — from lease grant to mineral dispatch, royalty
payment and compliance monitoring.

**Q: Who needs to register on i4MS?**
A: All lessees, licensees, crusher unit owners, and transporters dealing in minor
minerals in Odisha must register on the i4MS portal. Circle officers and
directorate staff are registered by the department.

**Q: Is there a mobile app for i4MS?**
A: Currently, i4MS is available as a web portal accessible from any browser.
Check-gate operators use a mobile-optimised scanner page for barcode verification.

## Leases

**Q: How do I apply for a new quarry lease?**
A: New quarry leases are granted through an auction process. Watch for auction
notices on the i4MS portal and the district collector's website. After winning
the auction, complete registration and upload required documents on the portal.

**Q: How long is a quarry lease valid?**
A: A quarry lease is granted for up to 5 years and may be renewed for a further
5 years. Apply for renewal at least 90 days before expiry.

**Q: What happens if my Environmental Clearance expires before my lease?**
A: Your lease remains legally active, but the system blocks new permit issuance
until EC is renewed. Existing permits and passes already issued continue until
their own validity ends.

**Q: Can I transfer my lease to another person?**
A: Transfer of a quarry lease requires prior written approval of the competent
authority. An application for transfer must be submitted on the portal with the
proposed transferee's details and a No Objection Certificate.

## Permits and Passes

**Q: How do I get an e-Permit?**
A: Log in to the i4MS portal, navigate to your lease, and click "Apply for
e-Permit (Form L)". Enter the quantity, consignee and destination. The system
runs automated clearance checks; if all pass, the permit is issued immediately.

**Q: Why was my permit application rejected?**
A: Common reasons: expired EC or CTO, overdue statutory returns, suspended lease,
or requested quantity exceeding remaining permit limit for the year.

**Q: How long is an e-Transit Pass valid?**
A: The validity window is set based on the distance between source and
destination. Typically 12 to 24 hours. Once expired, the pass cannot be reused.

**Q: My vehicle was flagged at a check gate. What do I do?**
A: Contact the circle DDM office with the pass ID and vehicle details. The DDM
will review the flag reason and either clear the flag or initiate further action.

## Payments

**Q: How do I pay royalty online?**
A: Log in to the portal, go to "Payments > Royalty", select the lease and
quarter, and pay using net banking, debit card, or UPI through the integrated
payment gateway.

**Q: What is the penalty for late royalty payment?**
A: Simple interest at 24% per annum on the outstanding amount from the due date.

**Q: What is the difference between royalty and dead rent?**
A: Royalty is based on the quantity of mineral dispatched. Dead rent is a fixed
amount payable when no mining is done. The lessee pays the higher of the two for
each quarter.

## Returns

**Q: When are statutory returns due?**
A: Monthly returns are due by the 5th of the following month. Annual returns are
due within 60 days of the end of the financial year.

**Q: What happens if I don't file returns on time?**
A: Returns overdue by more than 30 days may trigger a lease suspension. After 60
days, a show-cause notice for determination (termination) of the lease may be
issued.
